# wen-crypto.com — the front door to the Wen Almanac

**Site:** https://wen-crypto.com/ · **Full record:** https://wen-watt.github.io/

A small, animated community gateway to the story of Wen, the Solana cat meme that began as a poem and grew into an open NFT standard (WNS). Seven short sections — What Wen is, The record, Technology, Media & culture, Links, Privacy — a dancing cat in front of Jupiter, three quiz questions and a feed of all almanac posts. Every detail leads to the source-checked almanac.

The addresses of the former WordPress blog (2025–2026) are kept as redirect pages to the matching almanac posts, so old links keep working.

The one real WEN is the Solana mint `WENWENvqqNya429ubCdR81ZmD69brwQaaBYY6p3LCpk`. No prices, no forecasts, no exchange links, no trackers.

Community project, not the official Wen site — official: https://wenwencoin.com/. Educational content, not financial advice.
