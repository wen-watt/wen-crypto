Token-2022, WNS and Wen Open Lab: What Wen Built

Token-2022 is Solana's token program with extensions. WNS, the Wen New Standard, uses it to make NFTs light, open and fair to creators. Wen Open Lab turns both into free tools you can test: models, a till for shops and an AI skill.

Token-2022 in one minute

A token on Solana is a mint plus accounts. Token-2022 lets the mint carry extra rules — extensions — written into the token itself instead of into every app around it:

  • Metadata — name, symbol and picture stored with the mint.
  • Transfer Hook — a program runs on every transfer; WNS uses it to enforce royalties.
  • Non-Transferable — a soulbound token, used in the Lab's warranty receipts.
  • Permanent Delegate — an issuer can move or burn tokens; powerful, so always check who holds it.
  • Transfer Fee and Interest-Bearing — fees and yields defined at the token level.

WNS: the Wen New Standard

Proposed on January 25, 2024, WNS describes NFTs as Token-2022 mints with metadata and group pointers, plus a transfer hook that enforces creator royalties. The code is open in the Wen Program Library. Read the almanac guides: Token-2022 and WNS and SPL, Token-2022 and WNS explained.

Wen Open Lab: from models to working tools

  1. Receipts and warranty cards — a purchase proof that cannot be resold.
  2. Creator royalties — paid by the token on every sale.
  3. B2B invoices — invoices with rules the token enforces.
  4. A payments bridge — Solana Pay next to card acquiring.
  5. A payment simulator — card fees versus on-chain fees, side by side.
  6. Devnet — repeat everything yourself with free test coins.
  7. Wen Counter — a till in a web page on Solana Pay: it only receives, takes no fee per sale and is backed by 57 checks anyone can rerun.
  8. Shops: plugin and skill — the same till as a WooCommerce checkout, plus an AI skill that audits a shop and sets it up with the owner's consent.

The whole Lab also reads in Italian, Chinese and Korean. For AI assistants there is a hub of Wen Skills: one command, npx skills add wen-watt/skills.

Safety rule of the Lab: run models on devnet with a throwaway wallet; never paste a private key anywhere.

In some countries paying for goods with crypto assets is banned or needs a licence (for example Türkiye, Thailand, Indonesia, mainland China and Bangladesh). Check your local rules before you use Wen Counter.

Why this matters: the usefulness story

Everything commercial burns out; what lasts is what a token taught and built. For Wen that is three things you can check today: a launch that stress-tested Solana with more than a million wallets, a poem turned into a fractional NFT, and the Wen New Standard (WNS) — an open way to build NFTs on Token-2022. Wen Open Lab carries that standard into everyday tools, all free and all tested in the open: warranty receipts, royalties and invoices, a receive-only till any shop can open in a browser, a checkout for online shops and an AI skill that installs it safely. That is what the almanac keeps.
