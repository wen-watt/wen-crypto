The Wen Record: From a Poem to an Open Standard, 2023–2026

A short, dated record of what Wen did. Each line links to the full, source-checked post in the Wen Almanac.

Timeline

  • Late 2023 — Meow writes the Wen poem, “A Love Letter to Wen Bros”; the idea of a fractional-NFT community coin takes shape.
  • January 25, 2024 — WNS 0.0 is proposed on the Jupiter Research forum.
  • January 26, 2024 — WEN launches as the first LFG Launchpad token; the airdrop to over a million wallets becomes a live stress test for Solana.
  • January 29, 2024 — the claim closes and the unclaimed tokens are burned for good.
  • 2025 — culture grows: Wen x Bonk, Elevate Pictures (reported), the Copa de los Memes.
  • 2025–2026 — the wider rails mature: BSOL on the NYSE, tokenization pilots in Korea, U.S. stablecoin law.
  • 2026 — Wen Open Lab turns WNS into free, testable models anyone can run.

How the record is kept

Every post in the almanac carries a badge — Historical post, Reworked market post or Almanac guide — and a sources callout. All outside links live on one page, Sources and verification, so you can open, compare and decide for yourself. Claims we could not confirm are labelled “reported” and point to the official X account as the primary source.

Where the old posts went

This blog used to hold more than sixty posts. They have been rewritten, merged and checked in the almanac: prices and forecasts removed, technology and usefulness added. Browse them all in the almanac navigation.

Why this matters: the usefulness story

Everything commercial burns out; what lasts is what a token taught and built. For Wen that is three things you can check today: a launch that stress-tested Solana with more than a million wallets, a poem turned into a fractional NFT, and the Wen New Standard (WNS) — an open way to build NFTs on Token-2022. Wen Open Lab turns that standard into free, tested models: receipts and warranty cards, creator royalties, B2B invoices and a payments bridge. That is the story the almanac keeps.
