What Wen Is: the Poem, the Cat and the One Real Token

Wen is a Solana token whose supply is a poem. Meow, co-founder of Jupiter, wrote “A Love Letter to Wen Bros”; it was minted as an NFT and split into one trillion tokens — so holding WEN means holding a fraction of that poem.

Four facts that define Wen

  1. A poem as supply. The first community coin built on a fractionalized NFT.
  2. A launch as a test. WEN was the first token on Jupiter's LFG Launchpad and was airdropped to more than one million Solana wallets in January 2024 — the network stayed up.
  3. A burn for good. When the claim closed on January 29, 2024, the unclaimed allocation — more than 271 billion tokens, over 27% of supply — was burned on-chain.
  4. A standard. WEN is tied to the Wen New Standard (WNS), an open way to build NFTs on Token-2022 with royalties enforced by the token itself.

The one real WEN

Names and tickers can be copied; the address cannot. The only WEN is the Solana mint WENWENvqqNya429ubCdR81ZmD69brwQaaBYY6p3LCpk. Check it on the Solana Explorer before anything else, and read the trust chain to see which claims are verified, reported or unverified.

Why a word, and why a cat

“Wen” is the crypto spelling of “when” — the impatient question of every community. In Chinese, wén (文) means writing and culture, and wèn (问) means to ask. The almanac keeps the full history of the word. The cat is the hearth keeper of the story: friendly, free to draw and shared by everyone.

Why this matters: the usefulness story

Everything commercial burns out; what lasts is what a token taught and built. For Wen that is three things you can check today: a launch that stress-tested Solana with more than a million wallets, a poem turned into a fractional NFT, and the Wen New Standard (WNS) — an open way to build NFTs on Token-2022. Wen Open Lab turns that standard into free, tested models: receipts and warranty cards, creator royalties, B2B invoices and a payments bridge. That is the story the almanac keeps.
