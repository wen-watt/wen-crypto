What science knows so far

  • Meow melody carries mood: in a study of 780 meows from 40 cats, positive contexts mostly gave rising contours and negative ones falling contours (Schötz, van de Weijer and Eklund, Lund University). People with cat experience recognise the difference better.
  • Cats have 276 distinct facial expressions built from 26 movements; friendly ones often show closed eyes and forward ears (Scott and Florkiewicz, Behavioural Processes, 2023).
  • Adult cats rarely meow to each other: the meow is mainly a cat-to-human channel.
  • AI tools now turn meows into spectrograms and sort them by intent, and some apps claim very high accuracy; these claims are early and not independently confirmed.
