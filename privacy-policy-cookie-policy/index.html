Privacy, Cookies and Who Speaks Here

A short, plain policy for a small community site.

Who runs this site

wen-crypto.com is a community gateway to the Wen Almanac. It is not the official Wen project; the official site is wenwencoin.com. We sell nothing and run no advertising.

What data is collected

None by us. This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages: no accounts, no comments, no forms, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts. GitHub may keep standard server logs for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Not financial advice

Everything here is educational. Nothing is investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and nothing is an offer to buy or sell any asset. We do not publish prices or forecasts.

Content and credit

Texts are written by the almanac. Pictures come from the Wen community archive and are shared for non-commercial reuse with credit. If you see your work and want it credited differently or removed, write to the community on X.

Where this site came from

From 2025 to 2026 wen-crypto.com was a WordPress blog. Its posts were rewritten, checked and moved to the almanac; every old address now opens its new version.
